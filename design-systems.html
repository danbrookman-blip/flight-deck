Brand and design. Right look for the surface, every time.

We run two brands across three client-facing surfaces, plus Flight Deck for work that stays inside the company. Florence and the full design-system files hold the detail; the panels below show each look so you can recognise it on sight. The rule: pick by audience first. Going to a customer, use the brand the work belongs to. Staying internal, use Flight Deck. When it is still unclear, ask before you build.

Audience decides the surface. Going to a customer, use the brand the work belongs to: Airship or Toggle. Staying inside the company, a doc, deck, note, internal tool or this hub, use Flight Deck. It is the internal mark for Airship and Toggle work, and is never client-facing.
One name, two meanings. Inside Airship the in-app product line has historically been called Toggle as well. In this plan, "Toggle" means the usetoggle.com Hospitality Commerce brand (Surface C). The in-app look is the "Airship product" surface (B). When a request just says "Toggle", check which one is meant.
