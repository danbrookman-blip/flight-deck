There is no single right way to use Claude.

Use a quick chat for a one-off question or a fast draft. What Claude is to us

A capable assistant that takes a rough input and returns a clean, on-brand, structured output. It augments the tools we already use. It does not replace Fin in Intercom, Pipedrive, or Cursor.

Where your knowledge lives

Each department has one Brain: its knowledge base and the first place to look. There is a defined set, created and kept correct centrally, not hundreds of projects everyone spins up.

Who this is for

Everyone, on day one. Set up your profile, find your department Brain, and you are productive. The deeper craft comes in Part 2.
