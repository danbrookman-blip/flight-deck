01 · The Customer
Toggle ICP: Business Profile

The ideal Toggle customer is a recognisable UK hospitality operator with scale, seasonal ambition, and a gift card programme that's either underperforming or not yet properly built. They have existing infrastructure, a commercial team, and the beginnings of a gifting story. Toggle makes it properly commercial.

Business Characteristics

  • UK-based (primary) hospitality operator: restaurants, pub groups, hotel & dining, coffee chains, experiential venues
  • 50–500 sites; sweet spot 80–250 locations across one or multiple brands
  • Recognisable consumer brand with seasonal marketing activity
  • Existing gift card or voucher programme, likely on an inferior or generic provider
  • £5M+ in annual gift card / voucher revenue potential, or clear path to it
  • Multi-brand structure welcome, Toggle's group-level gifting handles separate branded webshops with cross-venue redemption

Performance Indicators

  • £20k+ weekly turnover per location, Toggle delivers strongest ROI at higher-revenue venues
  • Active seasonal peaks: Christmas, Valentine's, Mother's Day, Black Friday
  • Interest in physical gifting, on-demand print, and greetings gift cards as a margin-positive product layer
  • Corporate or B2B gifting demand: staff rewards, supplier incentives, client entertainment
  • Operating via Tillo or another B2B distribution network, or willing to activate one
  • Compatible EPOS system: Zonal, Oracle Symphony, or one of 50+ integrated partners

Technical Profile

  • Active EPOS integration, in-venue redemption is non-negotiable at enterprise scale
  • Own branded website or app with ability to embed Toggle webshop
  • Finance team requiring proper liability reconciliation and breakage reporting
  • API access appetite, enterprise operators often want Toggle embedded in their own digital estate
  • Open to Toggle POS Mode (tablet/mobile) for in-venue card sales if a till integration is not in place

Organisational Signals

  • Commercial Director, Head of Revenue, or CFO is the economic buyer
  • Marketing team runs seasonal campaigns, gifting is a commercial priority, not just a marketing one
  • Finance team actively manages gift card liability and breakage
  • Procurement team or framework, be ready with a fixed-fee commercial model and enterprise contract terms
  • IT or tech team who can facilitate EPOS setup and API work
  • Security awareness in procurement, expect a security review process at enterprise scale
02 · Buyer Personas
Who We're Selling To

Toggle enterprise has four buyer personas. The Commercial Director or CFO controls the decision. Marketing and Operations influence it. Security or IT may gate it at procurement. Each persona needs a distinct value narrative, the same pitch won't land with all four.

Primary Buyer · Economic Decision
Commercial Director / CFO

Owns the P&L for gift cards and experiences. Understands breakage as a revenue line. Wants predictable liability reporting, upfront cash capture, and a partner who can grow the gifting programme commercially.

Frustrated by: opaque reporting from current provider, inability to run promotions quickly, loss of breakage revenue to third-party platforms.

Cares About
  • Upfront cash from gift card sales
  • Breakage revenue visibility and capture
  • Fixed vs. commission cost certainty
  • Board-level gifting reports (Board Pack Booster)
  • B2B/corporate channel incremental revenue
Secondary Buyer · Marketing Influence
Head of Marketing / Marketing Director

Runs seasonal campaigns and wants a gifting platform that responds to their pace, launching a Black Friday flash sale or a Valentine's experience in hours, not weeks.

Frustrated by: slow time-to-market, lack of branded control, no campaign performance data, inability to integrate gifting with CRM.

Cares About
  • Speed of campaign launch
  • Brand control over webshop and cards
  • Promotional flexibility (added value, discounts, restricted validity)
  • Airship integration for CRM-driven gifting
  • Influencer gifting via Joli integration
Tertiary · Operational Influence
Head of Operations / IT Director

Needs the EPOS integration to work flawlessly across all sites. Manages the rollout of new venues onto Toggle. Wants self-service tools, not manual CS dependency.

Cares About
  • EPOS integration reliability and uptime
  • Speed of new site onboarding
  • Self-service restock and bulk order tools
  • API access for internal system integration
  • Finance reconciliation at group level
Gatekeeper · Procurement / Security
Head of IT Security / Procurement Lead

Appears later in the sales process. Their job is to reduce risk, but they can stall a deal if Toggle isn't prepared. At enterprise scale, expect a security or procurement review before contracts are signed.

Frustrated by: vendors who aren't prepared, missing documentation, unclear data handling policies.

Cares About
  • Data security and PCI compliance for card payments
  • GDPR handling and data residency
  • Vendor due diligence documentation
  • Integration security (API, EPOS data flows)
  • Business continuity and uptime SLAs
03 · Discovery
Questions to Confirm ICP Fit

These questions serve two purposes: confirming the opportunity is the right fit for Toggle enterprise, and beginning to build the commercial case. Use them to uncover pain at the right level, the CFO's pain, not just the marketing manager's.

  1. How many sites do you operate, and what does your growth plan look like over the next two years?
    Establishes size and trajectory. An 80-site group planning to reach 150 is a different conversation to one static at 80.
  2. What gift card or voucher programme do you have in place today, and who commercially owns it?
    Uncovers incumbent provider and who the real decision-maker is. If "marketing manages it," the CFO hasn't been engaged yet.
  3. What's your annual gift card revenue, and do you have visibility of breakage, cards sold but never redeemed?
    The breakage question often surfaces a gap: most operators have no visibility. This is CFO-level pain once uncovered.
  4. What EPOS system are you using across your estate: Zonal, Oracle Symphony, or something else?
    Confirms integration compatibility early. Important to establish before investing time in the commercial conversation.
  5. Do you currently sell gift cards through any B2B or corporate channels: staff rewards, Tillo, employee benefits platforms?
    Often untapped. Framing corporate gifting as a net-new revenue channel changes the commercial conversation.
  6. How quickly can you run a seasonal gifting campaign today, from decision to live?
    Surfaces frustration with the current provider. The answer is usually "weeks" or "we depend on our agency." Toggle's self-service speed is the counter.
  7. Who needs to be involved in a decision like this, and does it go through a procurement or security review?
    Maps the decision process early. Surfacing the security or procurement gatekeeper early prevents late-stage delays.
