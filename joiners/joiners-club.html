The Joiners Club.

A club with no card, no points and no app. Just a table when you want one and less noise the rest of the time.

The seven things we ask

Your name

So we can write to you, not to “valued customer”.

Your email

Two emails a month. Never more.

Your mobile

One text if your table is at risk, and nothing else. No marketing by text.

Your birthday

A table held for you and a glass on us. We will ask six weeks out, not the day before.

Your anniversary

The same again on the date that matters to you, whatever it marks.

Your postcode

We tell you what is on at your nearest place, and nothing about the other three.

Where you go most

You hear about your place, not all four.

What joining gets you, year one

Joining is rewarded, then it keeps being rewarded

Something on us the day you join, your birthday and your anniversary remembered, and a draw every month you are entered into by being a member. The rest is based on how often you actually come in, not on collecting points.

The day you join

A drink on us on your next visit, whichever place you walk into.

Your dates

Birthday and anniversary tables held, asked about six weeks out.

Every month

Entered into the members’ draw. Dinner for two, no forms to fill in.

The more you visit

Better held back for you, based on coming in rather than on points.
