Airship Actions

Every inbound enquiry and the moments that matter, surfaced as one prioritised inbox, then worked through a visual pipeline. Contact-us and party forms, follow-ups that fall due, deposits to chase, birthdays worth a call, and customers slipping from happy to unhappy, all in one place.

Prototype · live and clickable

Broadens enquiry management into an Actions platform. A website form lands in an Airship group and surfaces in the inbox; an agentic layer pushes the next action back when a first-response SLA is breached or a follow-up falls due. Click an enquiry into a drag-and-drop pipeline whose stages you define. Alongside the enquiries sit CRM triggers built from real contact data: a regular whose birthday is coming up and who always visits around it, a guest whose feedback has turned negative, and anyone entering a segment you choose to watch. At-risk and birthday actions link straight out to the contact's Airship profile.
