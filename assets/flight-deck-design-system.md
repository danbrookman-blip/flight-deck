# Flight Deck

> The internal home for Airship and Toggle. Runbooks, references, brand and decisions — staff only.

---

## Who we are

Airship and Toggle are sister hospitality-tech products, built in Sheffield (Airship since 2003, Toggle since 2018). We share customers, a sales team and a voice, while running on separate codebases and surfaces.

**Ownership today:** we were sold to Zonal in October 2022. Zonal was acquired by Volaris in February 2026, and Airship & Toggle now operate as a **new company within Volaris** — part of **Constellation Software**.

### The products

- **Airship** — a hospitality-only **CRM and email/SMS marketing platform**. It connects guest data across bookings, EPOS, WiFi, loyalty and gifting into one customer view, and its **Proof of Presence (PoP)** technology attributes real venue visits to marketing.
- **Toggle** — the **hospitality commerce and gifting platform**. Venues sell digital and physical gift cards, experiences, retail and merch in minutes, with flat per-location pricing and EPOS integration.

## What Flight Deck is

**Flight Deck** is our internal design language — the staff-only surface for documentation and tooling across both products. It is deliberately *not* the customer-facing product brand.

The governing idea: **internal docs are ink on warm paper, and colour is rationed.** Colour appears only to mark Airship vs Toggle and to carry semantic meaning (note / tip / caution / danger). That restraint is what signals a surface is Flight Deck — calm internal tooling, not the colourful customer product.

- **Voice** — plain, direct, lightly warm. We're colleagues, not a manual. Sentence case everywhere; UPPERCASE is reserved for monospace labels and IDs.
- **Type** — Hanken Grotesk for prose and UI; IBM Plex Mono for labels, IDs and code.
- **Colour** — warm off-white paper and near-black ink. **Airship = deep teal**, **Toggle = coral**. No gradients, no photography, no decoration beyond the dashed "perforation" rule borrowed from kitchen order tickets.
- **Logo** — the Flight Deck wordmark fuses the Airship and Toggle marks: purple *flight* + blue *deck* with a coral pivot dot.
