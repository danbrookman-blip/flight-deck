FD · PAR · 02

What partnerships means here

Partnerships at Airship and Toggle is not a directory listing. It is an active commercial and relational function, one that connects our ecosystem to real growth for operators.

01

Relationships first

Every partner is a person before they're a pipeline. We build relationships that last because we invest in them genuinely, not just when we need something.

02

Mutual value, always

We won't enter a commercial arrangement unless there's a clear, tangible gain for our operators. If it doesn't benefit the customer, it doesn't happen.

03

Commercially driven

This function is measured. SQLs, accreditations, deal attribution. Relationships are the engine; revenue is the outcome we track.

04

Quality over quantity

80+ integrations is a start, not a finish. We'd rather have 30 outstanding partnerships that generate consistent value than 150 names on a list.

FD · PAR · 04

The scoring framework

Partners are scored across four dimensions. The total score determines their tier. Scores are reviewed annually, or when the portfolio audit flags a change.

Rmax 3 pts

Reach

How many operators in our ICP use this platform? A partner used by 1,000+ mid-market UK hospitality venues scores higher than one with 50 customers.

Smax 3 pts

Selling power

Can this partner's team actively refer and sell Airship/Toggle to their customers? Do they have a partnerships or sales channel that touches our ICP?

Cmax 3 pts

Cross-platform relevance

Does the integration work across both Airship and Toggle? A partner live on both platforms scores higher than one integrated with only one.

Mmax 3 pts

Marketing value

What's the co-marketing potential? Webinars, case studies, joint content, event presence, social reach. Partners who actively promote the relationship score higher.

The partner journey

All new partners enter at Connected. Movement upward is earned through demonstrated commercial value, integration depth and co-marketing activity.

Step 01

First contact

You identify or are approached by a new technology partner. Qualification happens here.

Step 02 · Auto

Connected

Auto-assigned. Integration scoped and accreditation started. Commercial conversation begins.

Step 03

Core

Active referral pipeline established. Co-marketing underway. Regular cadence in place.

Step 04

Founding

Maximum reach, deepest integration, highest ACV potential. Full co-marketing partnership.

Caution · qualifying a new partner

There must be a tangible gain for our operators. A partner qualifies if they offer operators at least one of: (1) a place to redeem a Toggle gift card, (2) a place to sell a Toggle gift card, (3) a source of guest data to enrich the Airship CRM.

If none of these apply, we don't proceed commercially, regardless of their reach.

FD · PAR · 07 · Stage 1

Who pays the accreditation fee?

The accreditation fee always sits with the partner, but how it gets there depends on who initiated the request.

A

Partner approaches us

The partner comes to Airship or Toggle wanting an integration.

Who pays
The partner, directly.
Why
They want access to your ecosystem. The integration is the door; the accreditation fee is the cost of opening it. You're charging them to become a validated, promoted member of the partner network.
Script

"We'd love to work with you. Here's how our partnership accreditation works - it's a £2,500 one-time fee, and here's what that gets you..."

B

Customer requests the integration

A customer asks Airship or Toggle to integrate with a specific partner.

Who pays
Still the partner, but via the customer's ask.
How it works
We go back to the partner: "One of your customers wants this integration. We can build it - here's the accreditation fee." They decide whether to absorb the cost (likely, if the customer is significant) or push back.
Safeguard

This prevents Airship from building integrations on demand under customer pressure with no commercial return, a position that weakens our leverage.

FD · PAR · 08 · Stage 2

What every accredited partner gets

Once accreditation is complete, every partner receives Partnership Access at no ongoing cost. This is the baseline. It is not nothing, but it is not active commercial partnership.

Included · what they get

Partnership Access

  • Live integration. The Data Bridge is active. Their platform and Airship/Toggle communicate.
  • Academy listing. Published article in the Academy; customers can find and follow setup steps.
  • Integration directory. Listed as a compatible integration on the Academy collection page.
  • Technical support. Ant and the CS team support integration queries. The connection is maintained.
  • The accredited badge. They can state they're an accredited Airship/Toggle integration partner.
Pack only · what they don't get

Reserved for the Pack

  • Website partner page feature. No active listing on the partner directory.
  • Logo rights. Cannot use Airship or Toggle logos in their materials.
  • Database eshot or webinar. Not introduced to the operator base. No joint marketing.
  • Event presence. Logo not featured on stands or event materials.
  • Sales team referencing. Sales and CS aren't actively recommending them.
  • Referral reciprocity. The £10/location/month model is not activated.

FD · PAR · 11

The referral model

Simple, reciprocal and provable. £10 per location per month, both ways.

Airship refers → partner

The partner pays Airship £10/location/month for that customer.

Partner refers → Airship

Airship pays the partner £10/location/month for that customer.

The amounts naturally self-balance. The relationship drives the outcome.

The provability rule

A referred customer only qualifies if they were not in the referring party's active pipeline in the last 6 months. This prevents disputes and keeps the model clean.

The tracking requirement

All referrals must be logged in Pipedrive at the point of introduction, not retrospectively. Retroactive attribution is harder to evidence and subject to dispute resolution by Amy.

Pack partners only

Referral reciprocity is only activated for Partnership Pack partners. Access partners are not part of the £10/location model, it's an exclusive benefit of the commercial relationship.

FD · PAR · 12

Pipedrive: the source of truth

Everything flows through Pipedrive. If it isn't logged, it doesn't exist commercially, and it can't be attributed or paid.

01

Partner Lead Pipeline

All inbound leads from partners are logged here. Each lead is tagged to the referring partner organisation. This is how referral payments get triggered.

02

Partner Organisation Cards

Each partner has an organisation card showing tier, category, integration status, last contacted, next review date, warm/cold, pack status, and commercial agreements in place.

03

Deal attribution

When a partner-sourced lead converts, attribution must be recorded at point of opportunity creation and confirmed at close. Retroactive attribution is difficult and disputed.

04

Referral form

Partners submit referrals through a dedicated form. This creates a new Pipedrive entry automatically, tagged to the partner, removing manual input and keeping the trail clean.
