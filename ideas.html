Community Content Manager

Lets a multi-site manager submit one local story or charity appeal to every lounge they run in a single pass, instead of copy-pasting it into a spreadsheet for each site. Central marketing reviews, approves and schedules it into each site's own branded email. One submission reaches 8 sites and 32,360 regulars from a single screen.

Airship Lookout

Lets a floor manager look up what a guest can redeem from one mobile number or email and trigger a verification text the guest taps to authorise the redemption, instead of either refusing the request or peeking at customer records. Notes, random acts of kindness and known-guest heads-ups all live on one profile screen. One floor app removes the trust-versus-PII bind that keeps venue staff locked out of customer data today.

Customer-tap verification Notes with voice dictation Strava-style insights and push

Single Customer View

Puts every customer segment on one screen, ranked by what a single visit is worth, so a manager sees where the value sits at a glance. The headline number is spend per visit: Known Spend divided by Proof of Presence visits. Group the tiles into named views, drag them into the layout you want, and share a view with one link.
