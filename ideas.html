Ideas

Working prototypes, not slideware.

A home for product ideas built as clickable prototypes. Each one is a real, working demo you can open and try, with a build spec ready to hand to engineering. This page is the commercial rationale: why we prototype, what earns a build, and how an idea graduates.

Why a prototype, not a deck

A clickable demo proves an idea a slide cannot. It shows the real workflow, surfaces the hard parts early, and hands engineering a spec instead of a guess. Cheap to make, and honest about what a build would actually take.

What earns one

A real, repeated problem for a defined user. A plausible commercial return: new revenue, revenue retained, or time taken out. And a fit with what we already sell, so it strengthens Airship or Toggle rather than scattering focus.

When we build

When real users have tried the demo, the commercial case holds up against the effort to build it, and an owner will carry it. Ideas that do not clear the bar stay as prototypes, cheap to revisit when the case changes.
