Airship. Loud brand, calm product.

Airship runs two looks. The gradient-forward marketing brand carries hero, logos and AI features. The calm card-on-white product surface is the in-app admin UI. Pick by where the work lands, then follow the swatches and type below.

Surface A · Airship brand

Loud, gradient-forward, confident.

Where: marketing, hero, logos, AI features. Type: Montserrat 400 to 900. Wordmark: purple gradient, never blue. Use the gradient sparingly, hero only.

Surface B · Airship product (in-app)

Calm, soft-grey card-on-white.

Where: the in-app product and admin UI. Type: Lato. Cards become whiter on hover, not greyer. Restrained, competent, never cute.
