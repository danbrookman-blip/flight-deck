The infrastructure is built. The focus now is recruitment momentum and scaled growth.

  • Explains the Professionals Community and who it is for.
  • Showcases existing members, so professionals are in good company.
  • CTA to get in touch with Alex via a form that feeds straight into Pipedrive.
  • Links to the Professionals index for social proof.

Pipedrive workflow

FD · PRO · 02

Who we're recruiting

Quality over quantity. Deliberately recruited, not mass acquired.

The Community

Well-informed hospitality specialists with strong reputations, covering a range of skills within the marketing umbrella.

A trusted, visible network: people our operators can turn to for expertise, and people who can refer operators to us. They stay close to both businesses, informed and engaged.

Super Users

A deeper relationship with Airship and Toggle, and a deeper level of platform expertise.

Super Users help operators get the most out of our platforms, acting almost as account managers, hands-on in client work. They are trained, experienced, and actively using the tools to deliver measurable results.

Caution · route to sales

Route-to-market or promotional platforms are commercial partnership conversations, not community fits. These are handed directly to sales.

FD · PRO · 04

Content & visibility

Recruitment is not just outreach. Content convinces before any message is sent.

Professional spotlights

Not just introductions: rich forward pieces with direct quotes, case studies and real stats. Content that demonstrates the calibre and impact of our community.

Thought leadership

Co-created with professionals on the topics they know best. Their expertise, their voice, our platform.

Data-led insight

Professionals have access to real project data and sector statistics. Co-produced insight pieces, heavier on numbers, adding authority to the community and Airship.

Team marketing sponsorship

Signed agreement (~£25k) with the first sponsored post pointed directly at Professionals recruitment. Extends reach beyond our owned channels from day one.

Mixed media & co-content

Webinars, video, social takeovers and collaborative formats. Raises the professional's profile while building Airship and Toggle's authority across channels.

FD · PRO · 05

Keeping professionals close

Once recruited, professionals enter a structured cycle of involvement, staying genuinely informed, connected and valued by both businesses.

Early access

Professionals see major product developments before general release. Already demonstrated with Ask Airship. Continues with SCV, Segments, Performance Dashboard and future releases.

Drop-in sessions

A recurring, informal community session, not a formal webinar. Format rotates: product previews, industry discussion, open Q&A, feedback. Regular contact that feels accessible.

Training & upskilling

Platform training so professionals can actively recommend us. A clear path from community member to Super User for those who want deeper involvement.

Advocacy

Testimonials, success stories, social visibility and referrals. Professionals actively recommending the platform and community. Creating advocates, not just members.

FD · PRO · 07

Paid acquisition, when the engine is ready

Paid activity amplifies a proven recruitment process. It doesn't build awareness from scratch.

1

Organic & outreach first

Prove the message and identify what converts. LinkedIn outreach, warm referrals, events and content build a validated recruitment process.

2

Case studies & co-content

Professionals generate credibility through their own content and endorsements. A steady stream of co-created content and advocacy amplifies the network before paid spend begins.

3

Paid LinkedIn ads

Profession-based targeting. In-platform form fills. Landing page as destination. Introduced once the organic engine has matured and conversion is established.

Note · sequencing

Paid acquisition is a planned phase, not the current priority. Organic, outreach, partnerships and community content come first.

FD · PRO · 08

How we'll measure success

Measured as a commercial asset, not just a community metric.

Recruitment

  • Community members recruited
  • Super Users recruited
  • Applications received
  • Messages responded to, fit or not
  • Landing page conversion rate
  • Outreach conversion rates

Engagement

  • Drop-in session attendance
  • Webinar participation
  • Content collaborations
  • Testimonials secured
  • Professional referrals made
  • Social reach, engagement and shares

Commercial impact

  • Leads introduced to sales
  • Opportunities influenced
  • Revenue generated through the network
  • Pipeline contribution over time
