Contact Profile

Airship's Edit Contact screen, rebuilt into one working view: message a guest in two clicks, see every offer issued inline, and lead with what a guest is worth.

Prototype · live and clickable

Rebuilds the legacy Edit Contact page on Airship's product surface. Email or SMS a guest in two clicks, see every offer they have been issued inline rather than behind a link, and read the numbers that matter. The headline is spend per visit: total spend divided by Proof of Presence visits, so a manager sees what a guest is worth at a glance. Each interaction count greys out when it is zero, so the screen only highlights what actually happened.
