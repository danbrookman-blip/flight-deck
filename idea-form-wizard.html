Form Wizard Rebuild

Airship's create-a-form page, rebuilt as a guided five-step wizard: one clear task per step, with a live preview of the form updating as you build it.

Prototype · live and clickable

The current create-form page stacks over 40 settings onto one long scroll. This rebuild splits the same job into five ordered steps: Basics, Fields, Settings, Style, then Review and publish. A field builder adds and reorders what you collect, and a live preview panel shows the finished form as you type, so there is no guesswork before you publish. Built on Airship's current design system, purple and magenta, Montserrat and Inter.
