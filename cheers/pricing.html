Good to know

The common questions.

Is the price really just per location?
Yes. You pay one monthly fee for each venue, and the rate drops as your group grows, from £125 for a single site down to £20 a location at 101+ venues. There are no per-employee fees. Prices are subject to terms and conditions.
What happens when a team member joins?
They are enrolled automatically from your HR system, taken through the onboarding journey, issued their welcome reward and asked for feedback. Nothing to set up by hand.
And when someone leaves?
When they are marked as a leaver in your HR system, they are removed from Airship on the next daily sync. Their comms stop and their access to the staff rewards portal ends.
How do staff access their benefits?
Through a secure magic-link URL, with no password to remember and no app to download. They can save it to their phone home screen so it behaves like an app. Add to Wallet is coming soon for one-click access to individual rewards.
Will it work with our existing systems?
Cheers syncs with Zonal, Fourth, S4 Labour, RotaReady and Polaris, and connects to Toggle and Zonal loyalty for perks and discounts. A daily API sync keeps employee data accurate across all of them.
How long does it take to go live?
Around four weeks. We set up the account, switch on your integrations, build your templates and run training with your HR team, then your account manager supports you from launch onwards.
